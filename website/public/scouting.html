Plate 04 Scouting and the Route Schema 1

Candidates and one selection

The coordinator names the route.

Blind scouting hands each candidate an unattributed projection. The call is request_scouts, and the return is complete_scouts. A response outside the requested range, or one that contradicts itself, is rejected. The default fixture drive does not enter blind scout on its own. After a punt it assigns possession and returns to normal.

Effective yards

Every adjustment stays on the record so a route can be recomputed from the same inputs. One million ppm is a neutral multiplier. Milli-yard terms are the nominal distance, historical error, variance, and recovery cost.

calibrated = nominal + historical_error
blended    = calibrated + variance * (1 - calibration_confidence)
stretched  = blended / provider_reliability
effective  = stretched / (domain_suitability * capability_headroom) + recovery_cost

Ties break on an explicit priority, then on the route id. Calibration is stored per versioned model key, task class, and toolset.

What a candidate can see

On the packet

Evidence, open gates, and contradictions. Enough to estimate the work that remains.

With the coordinator

Predecessor distance, rankings, coordinator notes, model reputation, and the conversation. The packet scanner refuses those keys.
