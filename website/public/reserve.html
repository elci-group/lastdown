Plate 02 The Reserve Schema 1

Spend classes

Ordinary work stops at the hold.

Ordinary work may spend remaining − reserve_hold. Continuation work may spend the hold, and only while a punt, a reset, or a field goal is being sealed. A provider boundary with that hold intact commits a punt. MachineEvent::Unrecoverable(ProviderExhausted) returns ExhaustionIsNotTurnover.

When lastdown opens

should_open_lastdown stays closed while ordinary resources cover the remaining work. It opens when ordinary resources do not cover the next cost. Otherwise it opens when pressure is already at lastdown, banked utility is above zero, and a fresh ordinary grant covers the work that remains.

A possession that can still cover the remainder keeps the ball.

Four utilities

Decision order starts at punt. A later choice replaces it only when its utility is strictly greater.
ChoiceUtility
Attemptscale_by_probability(p_score, full_utility). Zero when p_score sits under min_attempt.
Field goalThe banked utility, taken as it stands.
PuntPreserve-probability applied to banked utility plus the scaled remainder.
ResetThe punt utility times (1_000_000 + reset_bonus_ppm) / 1_000_000.

Equal punt and reset utilities stay on punt. A field goal seals through the punt transaction, then the status returns to field goal, which is terminal, so the drive does not resume. The case is hopeless when banked utility and all four utilities are zero.

Drive statuses

  1. kickoff
  2. normal
  3. redzone
  4. lastdown
  5. attempt
  6. field goal
  7. reset
  8. punt
  9. blind scout
  10. route
  11. score
  12. turnover

Field goal, score, and turnover are terminal. On the default drive a punt or a reset resumes by assigning possession and returning to normal. The fixture runner does that handoff itself. Blind scouting stays on the coordinator API, request_scouts and complete_scouts.

Repeated possessions

loop_signal reads the progress marks. Two regressions ask for rollback. Six marks with no positive advance and no score ask for diagnosis. Three neutral marks ask for reassessment. Rollback outranks diagnosis, and diagnosis outranks reassessment. The campaign loop stops at 32 cycles. Kill::ImmediatelyAfterCommit stops after one committed punt.

Fixtures set reserve_hold explicitly and lock the reserve, so the punt point stays exact. An unlocked budget follows estimate_reserve as the field grows.
