Plate 01 The Field Schema 1

Ownership

The field belongs to the orchestration.

Yards-to-go are model-relative. The field itself is independent of whichever model currently holds the ball. The coordinator owns the scoreboard: rankings, effective distance, and the selected route stay with ordinary code.

What the ledger keeps

The in-process journal in src/padagonia.rs records observations, assertions, evidence, contradictions, corroborations, and supersessions. Both sides of a contradiction stay on the record. Scalar gate distance is the authoritative field distance. A compact integer vector is a size comparison, and VECTOR_REQUIRED_FOR_RESUME is false, so resume proceeds from the scalar record.

Athletic amnesia

A candidate packet carries evidence, gates, and contradictions. The continuation scanner refuses competitive and conversational keys, so a successor does not inherit the predecessor’s distance, the rankings, the coordinator’s notes, a model key, or the conversation.

Reference

Parts per million. scale_by_probability returns 0 when the ppm or the value is 0, and otherwise max(1, ppm * value / 1_000_000).
